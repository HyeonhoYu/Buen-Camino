Seven stretches

The French Way, from Roncesvalles to Santiago, split into seven stretches. Each one teaches what you will really run into on that part of the trail, and every lesson you finish earns a stamp in your credencial.

  1. Roncesvalles to PamplonaOpen
    Sounds and spelling: vowels, consonants, stress, and accent marks
  2. Pamplona to LogroñoComing soon
    Greetings, introducing yourself, numbers, money
  3. Logroño to BurgosComing soon
    Ordering food, the pilgrim menu, saying what you like
  4. The Meseta, Burgos to LeónComing soon
    Asking the way, time, weather, explaining how you feel at the pharmacy
  5. León to PonferradaComing soon
    Booking a bed, solving problems, talking about what you did today
  6. Ponferrada to SarriaComing soon
    Shopping, comparing, Galicia's two languages
  7. Sarria to SantiagoComing soon
    Plans and feelings, writing your own pilgrim story

Trail companions

Each friend's name hides one of the sounds you learn in the first stretch.

Lucía, the Iberian lynx

Sharp ears, so she handles pronunciation. Her name has Spain's c sound and an accent mark.

Ramón, the donkey

Carries the packs and never hurries. When you miss something, he tells you why.

Begoña, the stork

Watches the trail from church bell towers and brings you stories about each region.

How it works

One lesson a day, ten minutes

Each lesson goes: listen, pick the letter, say it, fill in the vowels, get your stamp. If you did not earn all three steps, walk the same lesson again tomorrow.

The Spanish of Spain

Pronunciation and vocabulary follow Spain. Sounds come from your device's Spain Spanish (es-ES) voice, and recorded voices will be added over time.

Speaking practice works best in Chrome

Checking what you say works best in Chrome, Edge, and Chrome on Android. In other browsers, listen and compare on your own.

No sign-up, no ads

Progress is saved only in this browser on this device. If you switch devices, your progress stays behind.

Support Buen Camino with a coffee
